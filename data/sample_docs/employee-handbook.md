# Employee handbook (sample)

## Time off

Employees request paid time off (PTO) through the HR portal at least **10 business days** before the start date. Managers must approve within **3 business days**. Emergency leave can be requested with same-day notice when documented with HR.

## Remote work

Hybrid employees are expected to be in-office **Tuesday and Thursday** unless an exception is approved by their director.

## Expenses

Submit receipts within **30 days** of purchase. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
